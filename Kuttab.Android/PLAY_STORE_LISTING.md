## Full Description (4000 characters max)

Kuttab is a comprehensive Tajweed training application designed to help you master the rules of Quranic recitation. Whether you're a beginner or advanced student, Kuttab provides an interactive way to learn and practice Tajweed rules with authentic Quranic text and professional recitations.

**🎯 Key Features:**

📖 **Tajweed Rule Search**
• Search for specific Tajweed rules throughout the entire Quran
• Browse by categories: Noon Sakinah, Meem Sakinah, Madd, Qalqalah, and more
• View all occurrences of each rule with highlighted text
• Learn through practical examples from the Quran

🎧 **Audio Recitations**
• Listen to professional recitations by renowned Qaris
• Multiple reciters available: Abdul Basit, Al-Husary, Ayman Sowaid, and more
• Repeat individual verses for practice
• Play entire Surahs or custom verse ranges
• Teacher mode (Muallim) for learning pronunciation

🔍 **Flexible Search Options**
• Search the entire Quran (all 114 Surahs)
• Focus on a single Surah
• Search within a range of Surahs
• Find multiple occurrences within verses

📱 **Offline Support**
• Download audio files for offline use
• Download verse images for offline viewing
• Works without internet connection once content is downloaded

🌐 **Multi-Language Support**
• Arabic (العربية)
• English
• German (Deutsch)
• More languages coming soon

⚙️ **Customizable Settings**
• Choose your preferred reciter
• Adjust font sizes for comfortable reading
• Select search domains
• Configure online/offline resources

🎓 **Educational Purpose**
Kuttab is designed as a training tool to complement your Tajweed studies. It helps you identify and practice Tajweed rules throughout the Quran, making it easier to memorize and apply these rules in your recitation.

**📚 Content Sources:**
• Quranic text: Tanzil.net (Uthmani script)
• Audio recitations: EveryAyah.com
• Verse images: Standard Madani Mushaf format

**⚠️ Important Notice:**
This application is for educational purposes only. It cannot replace learning Tajweed with a qualified Sheikh. Proper Tajweed requires direct guidance from certified teachers.

**🕌 Support a Good Cause:**
The app includes an optional donation feature to support the BBF Community Center project in Freiburg, Germany. Your contribution helps build educational and community facilities.

**🔒 Privacy:**
Kuttab respects your privacy. We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All settings are stored locally on your device. Internet access is only used for streaming audio and images when enabled.

## Testing Instructions for Google Review Team

1. Launch the app
2. Select a language (English recommended for review)
3. Go to the main search tab
4. Select a Tajweed rule from the dropdown (e.g., "Idgham" under "Noon Sakinah")
5. Click "Search" to see results
6. Select a verse from the results
7. Click "Play" to hear the audio (requires internet or downloaded files)
8. Navigate to Settings to see configuration options
9. Navigate to Recitation tab to test recitation mode
10. Navigate to Info tab to see app information and credits

**Note**: Audio streaming requires internet connection. If audio doesn't play, enable "Get Audio Files from Internet" in Settings.
