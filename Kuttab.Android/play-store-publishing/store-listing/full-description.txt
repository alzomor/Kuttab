Kuttab is a comprehensive Tajweed training application designed to help you master the rules of Quranic recitation. Whether you're a beginner or advanced student, Kuttab provides an interactive way to learn and practice Tajweed rules with authentic Quranic text and professional recitations.

Key Features:

Tajweed Rule Search
- Search for specific Tajweed rules throughout the entire Quran
- Browse by categories: Noon Sakinah, Meem Sakinah, Madd, Qalqalah, and more
- View all occurrences of each rule with highlighted text
- Learn through practical examples from the Quran

Audio Recitations
- Listen to professional recitations by renowned Qaris
- Multiple reciters available: Abdul Basit, Al-Husary, Ayman Sowaid, and more
- Repeat individual verses for practice
- Play entire Surahs or custom verse ranges
- Teacher mode (Muallim) for learning pronunciation

Audio Recording
- Record your own recitation for each Ayah
- Ayah-specific recording with organized storage
- Playback your recordings to practice and improve
- Delete and re-record as needed
- Recordings saved with Sura_Ayah naming format

Flexible Search Options
- Search the entire Quran (all 114 Surahs)
- Focus on a single Surah
- Search within a range of Surahs
- Find multiple occurrences within verses

Offline Support
- Download audio files for offline use
- Download verse images for offline viewing
- Works without internet connection once content is downloaded

Multi-Language Support
- Arabic, English, German
- More languages coming soon

Notifications
- Push notifications for app updates and news
- Stay informed about new features and content
- Optional notification settings

Customizable Settings
- Choose your preferred reciter
- Adjust font sizes for comfortable reading
- Select search domains
- Configure online/offline resources

Educational Purpose: Kuttab is designed as a training tool to complement your Tajweed studies.

Content Sources: Quranic text from Tanzil.net, Audio from EveryAyah.com

Important Notice: This application is for educational purposes only. It cannot replace learning Tajweed with a qualified Sheikh.

Privacy: We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. All settings are stored locally on your device.
