# Privacy Policy for Kuttab - Tajweed Trainer

**Last Updated: January 14, 2025**

## Introduction

Kuttab ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Tajweed Trainer application handles information when you use our mobile application.

## Information We Collect

### Information We DO NOT Collect

Kuttab does NOT collect, store, or transmit any personal information. Specifically:

- We do NOT collect your name, email address, or contact information
- We do NOT collect your location data
- We do NOT collect device identifiers
- We do NOT track your usage patterns
- We do NOT use analytics or tracking services
- We do NOT collect any personally identifiable information (PII)

### Information Stored Locally

The app stores the following data locally on your device only:

- **App Settings**: Your language preference, selected reciter, search domain settings, and display preferences
- **Downloaded Content**: Quran audio files and images that you choose to download for offline use

This data is stored only on your device and is never transmitted to our servers or any third parties.

## Internet Permissions

The app requests internet permissions for the following purposes only:

1. **Audio Streaming**: To stream Quran recitations from EveryAyah.com when "Get Audio Files from Internet" is enabled
2. **Image Loading**: To load Quranic verse images from EveryAyah.com when "Get Aya Images from Internet" is enabled
3. **Donation Links**: To open external donation pages (PayPal) when you choose to support the BBF Center project

**Important**: Internet access is optional. The app works fully offline if you have downloaded the required audio and image files locally.

## Third-Party Services

### Content Sources

The app accesses the following third-party services only when you enable online features:

1. **EveryAyah.com**: For streaming Quran audio recitations and verse images
   - No personal data is sent to this service
   - Only standard HTTP requests for audio/image files

2. **PayPal**: For donations (optional)
   - Opens external browser for donation processing
   - Subject to PayPal's privacy policy
   - We do not receive or store any payment information

### No Third-Party Analytics or Advertising

We do NOT use:
- Google Analytics or any analytics services
- Advertising networks
- Social media tracking
- Crash reporting services that collect personal data

## Data Security

Since we do not collect or transmit any personal data:
- Your privacy is protected by design
- All app settings are stored locally on your device
- No data is transmitted to our servers
- No account creation or login is required

## Children's Privacy

Kuttab is suitable for all ages. Since we do not collect any personal information, we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 or any other age group.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by:
- Updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy
- Posting the new Privacy Policy in the app and on our website

## Data Deletion

Since we do not collect or store any personal data on our servers, there is no data to delete. To remove all locally stored data:

1. Uninstall the app from your device
2. Or clear the app's data through your device settings

## Consent

By using Kuttab, you consent to this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

## Disclaimer

Kuttab is an educational tool for learning Tajweed. It is not a substitute for learning from a qualified Sheikh. The app provides:

- Quranic text from Tanzil.net (Uthmani script)
- Audio recitations from EveryAyah.com
- Tajweed rule patterns for educational purposes

We strive for accuracy but recommend verification with qualified scholars.
